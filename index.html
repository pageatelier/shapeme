THE SILUA POINT OF VIEW

Shape,
not shrink.

Your body is not a number to chase.

Body recomposition asks a different question: what do you want to build? SILUA puts progressive resistance training at the center — so the goal is not simply to weigh less, but to create strength, definition and proportion over time.

Not less of you. More intention.

TRAINING, NOT RESTRICTION

Don't make “eat less”

FAQ

The useful
questions.

Is SILUA a weight-loss app?+

No. SILUA is built around body recomposition through resistance training. Your goals may include reducing body fat, but the training system is designed around strength, muscle and shape rather than making the scale the center of the experience.

Can training really change my silhouette?+

You cannot change your bone structure, but resistance training can develop muscle and change body composition. SILUA intentionally prioritizes glutes, shoulders, back, legs and core because those areas contribute to overall proportion and shape.

What if my gym does not have an exercise?+

SILUA uses equipment-aware substitutions within the same movement pattern so the structure of your program stays intact while the exercise adapts to what you can actually use.

Is it beginner friendly?+

Yes. Exercise selection and starting targets can adapt to training experience. The goal is to remove programming guesswork, not add more of it.
