FAQ

A few things
to know.

Is SILUA only for experienced gym users?+

No. SILUA is designed for gym beginners through intermediate users who want structure without having to learn programming first.

Do I need a fully equipped gym?+

No. Your plan takes your available equipment into account and uses close movement-pattern substitutions when needed.

Is this a weight-loss app?+

No. SILUA is centered on body recomposition, strength and shape. Weight can be one data point, but it is not the whole goal.

How many days a week can I train?+

SILUA supports 2, 3, 4 or 5 training days so your program can match the week you can realistically sustain.

Does SILUA replace a trainer or medical professional?+

No. SILUA provides fitness guidance and structured training support. Pain, injury or medical concerns should be addressed with an appropriate qualified professional.
