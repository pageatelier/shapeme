REAL COACHING, REMOTELY

They train on their time.
You coach the moments that matter.

SILUA is not built around scheduled video calls. It is asynchronous personal coaching built around a client's Training Profile, real workout history and real training videos.

01

Coach the training, not the calendar.

Review what clients actually did and respond with feedback or program changes when the training calls for it.

02

Coach across language barriers.

SILUA helps translate communication so clients and coaches can work in the language they are most comfortable using.

03

Coaching skill over follower count.

SILUA reviews professional background, experience and practical coaching ability — not social following.

HOW COACHING WORKS

Not just a program.
Coaching that follows the training.

01

A client chooses you

Clients compare specialties, coaching style, products, pricing and your profile.

02

Review their Training Profile

See their goal, experience, focus areas, frequency, session time, equipment and areas of discomfort.

03

Build & publish the program

Set workout days, exercises, order, sets, reps, rest, load guidance and coach cues.

04

They train for real

Clients follow the program in SILUA Today and log their sets, reps and load.

05

Receive a set

They send a training video with a question about form, ROM, activation, load selection or execution.

06

Coach & adjust

Give corrections, cues and a next-time action. Update the program when training needs to change.

SILUA VERIFIED

Not the most famous coach.
A coach clients can trust.

Before a coach becomes public, SILUA reviews their basic identity, professional background and the abilities needed to deliver real coaching through the platform.

01

Identity

Basic identity and real-name verification.

02

Professional Credential

Review of PT/strength credentials or other verifiable professional background.

03

Experience

Review of coaching history and relevant professional experience.

04

Coaching Assessment

A sample program or training-video feedback assessment may be used to review practical coaching ability.

Instagram, YouTube, a website and client testimonials can be submitted as optional portfolio material. Follower count itself is not a SILUA Verified standard.

WHO CAN APPLY

Coaches who help people
train better in real life.

We are looking for strength and fitness professionals who can design appropriate programs and give clear, responsible feedback around the client in front of them.

01Professional strength/fitness credential or reviewable professional background

02Individual or group coaching experience

03Ability to build and adjust training programs

04Ability to review training video and communicate clear feedback

05Responsible coaching within professional scope and safety standards
